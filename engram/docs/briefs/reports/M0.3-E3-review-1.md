## Assessment of the report's findings and open questions

The report numbers 15 findings (the brief said 9) and 5 open questions. Every factual claim I re-ran held, except
finding 4's "needs a superuser" (F15) and the latency figures, which were lower here (p99 2 to 8 µs).

1. Deterministic detail bytes: confirmed and pinned (mutation fails `TestDetailBytesAreDeterministic` 3/3).
2. Reject, not clamp: agreed, N11 and §4.1.2 bind; the §8.2 `internal/api` row also needs the text fix; the 50 %
   tolerance is F11.
3. `ns_group` storage: the `profile ->> 'group'` reading is a reasonable interim (profile changes are logged and
   notified, so a group change invalidates caches); it needs a ruling and a `Namespace.group` proto field.
   Recommendation: keep it in `profile` with a CHECK that `profile->'group'` is a string when present.
4. Grants: confirmed (`GRANT … ON ALL TABLES` as `catalog_migrate` fails on `goose_db_version`, `ON ALL FUNCTIONS`
   on `catalog_replicated`); the named grants are equivalent for the catalog's objects. Suggest
   `ALTER DEFAULT PRIVILEGES FOR ROLE catalog_migrate` so later migrations keep the reference's intent. Superuser
   claim: F15.
5. Additive inputs: accepted; no §2.2.3 signature changed (only struct fields were added).
6. Scope of `catalog.Postgres`: agreed; the other capabilities belong to the milestones that serve their RPCs.
7. NOT_FOUND{TENANT} for another tenant's tenant-level call: agreed, consistent with N5.
8. Only Recall and Retain metered: agreed (D13 names those two buckets).
9. Closed policy table: agreed.
10. Token shape: agreed, but see F3 (a tenant-less token with operator and tenant.admin).
11. UNAVAILABLE without a key set: agreed; F19.
12. Flush ends the down state: agreed.
13. Merge notes: `git merge-tree` against current `main` conflicts only in `go.mod`/`go.sum`; the pgtest
    duplicate is F4.
14. `make test-prop`: fails on the branch as reported; fixed on current `main` (`a1f7560`), moot after a rebase.
15. No `shard` trigger: confirmed; harmless for the Resolver today.

Open questions: (1) `profile.group` plus a proto field, as above. (2) Reject confirmed; tolerance per F11.
(3) NOT_FOUND. (4) No Reflect bucket. (5) With the first milestone that serves each API.
