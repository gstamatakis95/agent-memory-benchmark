## The worker's new open question (delete retries after the ack)

The problem is real and comes from the plan. N171(3) and N184 make `DeleteNamespace`'s `deleting` row and
`DeleteTenant` client-acknowledged writes. On a replication timeout the RPC answers `UNAVAILABLE{RetryInfo 2 s}`, and
§4.1.6 tells the client to "retry with the same `request_id`; the retry finds the row and re-checks". The barrier,
though, refuses every method on a `deleting` namespace or tenant except the three `AllowDeleting` ones (N70, §4.1.9).
So the documented retry gets `FAILED_PRECONDITION{NAMESPACE_DELETING | TENANT_DELETING}` and never reaches the
idempotency lookup. The two rules contradict each other; following the exemption list literally is defensible for this
branch, since no handler exists yet.

Recommendation for the ruling: give `DeleteNamespace` and `DeleteTenant` `AllowDeleting` in the policy. Their handlers
then run the `request_id` lookup first: a replay returns the stored response, or re-checks replication and answers
UNAVAILABLE again. Any other call answers the precondition itself. Record it in CONFLICTS.md with N70, N171(3) and
N184 before the first milestone that serves those RPCs.
